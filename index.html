Cannabis Decoder

Decode the product before you use it.

Enter as much or as little as you know. If you only have the strain name, the decoder still gives a helpful readout. Add your state first, then enter as much or as little as you know. Product type, variety, THC, terpenes, and amount make the readout more specific.

Educational only. This tool gives a quick consumer-style interpretation. It does not recommend a dose, diagnose a condition, or replace medical or legal advice.

About Cannabis Basics

Serious information. Mellow delivery.

Cannabis Basics is an education and resource hub built around a simple idea: people deserve a clearer way to understand products, learn the vocabulary, separate evidence from hype, and keep track of their own experiences.

The journal is the first physical product. The larger platform can grow into practical tools, short videos, research-informed articles, free worksheets, and digital resources—all with the same calm visual identity.
