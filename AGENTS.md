# Cannabis Basics — project rules (2026-10-27)

## Governing business decision
Cannabis Basics is a FREE educational website. Do not introduce paid memberships, founding-spot scarcity, paid signup banners, recurring fees, or a future paid tier without explicit new authorization. The printed Full Color Logbook on Amazon can remain an optional separately sold physical product.

## Access and data
Keep public articles, Decoder, Locator, FAQ and educational tools freely available. Private saved journals, sessions, inventory, wishlists, favorites, and saved dispensaries may use FREE accounts for secure storage. Do not delete accounts, alter existing private records, auth, payment records, RLS, or change privileged database functions without review and testing. Check any access limitations before claiming that new account signup works.

## Email / popups
Gift request: name and email, at least 25 seconds active engagement; unlock only after confirmed successful service acceptance. Magazine: publication is **Cannabis Basics Magazine**; distinct realistic cover, at least 120 seconds active engagement, separate affirmative consent for seasonal magazine and occasional promotional emails. Do not claim an issue exists before publication. Keep at least 60 seconds between popups; don't interrupt forms, journal use, checkout, or focused tool use. Show each automatic offer at most once per session. Suppress completed offers. All signup data must record source, timestamp, type, and precise consent words. Never equate downloading a gift with magazine consent.

## Infrastructure
Destination inbox: cannabisbasics@gmail.com. Prefer connected existing Supabase and permitted low-cost/no-cost email infrastructure; provider policies must allow cannabis-related education and promotions before activation. No new paid accounts or email campaigns unless explicitly authorized. Never expose private keys client-side. Do not activate offers with unverified endpoints or unapproved assets.

## Design and scope
Preserve green branding, current approved CB mark, navigation, journal tools, age-related notices, installed PWA, content, sounds, and working access behavior. Work ONLY in cannabisbasics/cannabis-basics unless specifically authorized. No cross-brand edits. Before edits review this AGENTS.md. Report actual tests, known blockers, GitHub commits and live URL. Avoid claiming deployment/browser success without verification.
