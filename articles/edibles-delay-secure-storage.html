Edibles, Delayed Effects and Safer Storage: A Household Checklist

The packaging may resemble an ordinary snack. Clear identification and secure storage matter before anyone opens it.

Cannabis Basics · Source links checked October 9, 2026 · Published October 10, 2026

What the source says

CDC notes that edible effects can take 30 minutes to two hours to appear and can be unpredictable. Cannabis products should be kept out of sight and reach of children and pets, ideally in secure locked storage. A delayed effect is not evidence that a product did nothing.

Put the information to work

Audit your household: original packaging, closed container, locked location, away from ordinary snacks. In the United States, Poison Control is 1-800-222-1222; call 911 for a medical emergency.
