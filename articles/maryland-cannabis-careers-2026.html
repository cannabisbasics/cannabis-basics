Cannabis Careers in Maryland: Turn an Industry Event Into a Learning Plan

The state’s October 10 workforce conference highlights a broader question: which skills transfer into cultivation, processing, retail and supporting roles?

Cannabis Basics · Source links checked October 9, 2026 · Published October 10, 2026

What the source says

Maryland Cannabis Administration lists its industry workforce conference for October 10, 2026, from 10 a.m. to 2 p.m. at the Maryland Innovation Center in Columbia. The program includes workshops, networking and a job fair covering cultivation, processing, retail and ancillary roles. Check the official page for event updates and registration details.

The second question leads to a better conversation: what does the number count, which period does it cover, and which conclusion would go too far?
