Maryland’s 2026 Cannabis Tax Numbers: Where the Revenue Comes From

A dated look at the second-quarter report, with a regional chart and a quick lesson in separating tax revenue from retail sales.

Cannabis Basics · Source links checked October 9, 2026 · Published October 10, 2026

What the source says

Maryland reported $28,685,226 in adult-use cannabis sales and use tax revenue for April–June 2026. The adult-use tax rate is 12%, following an increase from 9% on July 1, 2025. Tax receipts are not the same measure as the dollar value of all cannabis sales.
