Cannabis Recalls: Why the Batch Number Matters More Than the Brand

A Maryland recall from September 2025 shows how to match a notice to a specific product rather than guessing from a brand name.

Cannabis Basics · Source links checked October 9, 2026 · Published October 10, 2026

What the source says

A September 2025 Maryland notice recalled specified Hi Color Max Chocolate Milk products after coliform testing concerns. The notice identified affected production batches and package numbers. This historical case illustrates traceability; it is not a claim that every product from the brand is recalled or that this is today’s newest notice.

Put the information to work

Save a photograph of the product label and batch identifier with your receipt. If a notice matches, follow the regulator’s instructions and contact the licensed retailer; do not invent your own disposal method.

Try explaining the central idea to someone without using the headline as your conclusion. Name the exact measure, product category or decision first. Then identify the detail you would need before taking the next step.

For example, imagine two friends comparing a package, news story or personal observation. One points to the largest number; the other asks where it came from. The second question leads to a better conversation: what does the number count, which period does it cover, and which conclusion would go too far?
